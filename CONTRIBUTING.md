# Contributing

Feedback is welcome from developers, designers, accessibility practitioners, and especially people who encounter barriers in real products.

You do not need to submit code. An issue describing confusing language, a missing requirement, or a harmful assumption is a valuable contribution.

## Sharing a barrier

Include only what you are comfortable sharing. Helpful context can include:

- the page, pattern, or checklist item;
- what you expected to happen;
- what happened instead;
- browser, operating system, or assistive technology when relevant;
- a suggested improvement, if you have one.

No one owes this project personal medical information or unpaid product testing.

## Pull requests

Keep changes focused and explain:

- what changed;
- who benefits;
- what evidence supports the change;
- how the wording or example was verified.

Avoid claims of universal accessibility or complete conformance without the evidence to support them.

## Conduct

Be specific, patient, and willing to be corrected. Lived experience is not a debate prompt. Harassment, dismissal, or demands for personal disclosure are not welcome.
